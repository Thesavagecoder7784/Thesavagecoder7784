## Featured

⚽ **[xResidual](https://github.com/Thesavagecoder7784/xResidual)**
Pre-registered study of how Kalshi and Polymarket priced the 2026 World Cup, benchmarked against the sharp bookmaker line. Eleven falsifiable predictions committed to a timestamped commit before kickoff, graded publicly on Jul 19 (6 pass / 2 fail / 3 inconclusive). Millisecond-stamped in-play order-book capture on a 24/7 collector across 86 matches. Headline result: Polymarket leads cross-venue price discovery (~81% Gonzalo-Granger information share, 61 of 63 cointegrated matches), but the lead is 0% harvestable at size. The book collapses to ~0.5% depth at each goal, so the stale-quote gap is adverse selection observed in real time, not alpha. 115 tests, content-hash provenance, every figure reproducible from a clean clone.

🏎️ **[QuantF1](https://prabhatm27.medium.com/the-alpha-of-a-driver-separating-skill-from-machinery-in-formula-1-ebe0f9c099dd)**
Hierarchical Bayesian attribution of driver vs. car in Formula 1 across 76K fuel-corrected race laps (2022–25). Decomposes points variance into ~70% pace / 26% conversion / 4% luck, isolates conversion as a measurable within-team skill, and reports the harder half, absolute cross-team driver skill, as only weakly identified (AKM limited-mobility). States plainly what the data cannot support rather than forcing a point estimate. Paper in revision, targeting stat.AP (Fall 2026). Repo opens on submission.
